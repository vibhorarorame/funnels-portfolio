FAQ

Questions, answered

What is a funnel system?

Instead of one standalone funnel, it is a connected journey: a low-ticket offer, order bump, upsells, a webinar and automation that work together to turn a visitor into a paying high-ticket client.

Why start with a low-ticket offer?

Someone who pays $7 to $47 is far more likely to buy again than someone who downloads a freebie. The front-end revenue also helps cover your ad costs.

Which platform do you build on?

Mostly GoHighLevel, because pages, checkout, CRM, email, SMS and calendars live in one place. I can also work with your existing stack.

How long does it take?

A complete system takes about 30 days. A single funnel can be live in around 14 days once your content and brand assets are in.

Do you run ads too?

My focus is the system and automation. I set up tracking and work alongside your ads team so every step is measured.
